# Project Summary

Usekeyframes is a Laravel 12 + Inertia/Svelte application for AI-assisted video editing with keyframe animation, subtitles, and production-ready exports.

## Audience
Creators, marketers, founders, product teams, and small businesses that need polished videos faster.

## Content System
- Source of truth for Blade SSR SEO content: `config/seo.php`.
- Live routes: `/use-cases/{slug}` and `/blog/{slug}`.
- Draft SEO entries may be staged in `draft_programmatic_pages` until reviewed for publication.
